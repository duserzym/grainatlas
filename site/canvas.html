Grains and spots

Find the grains, make the final call by hand and place laser spots.

Find and review grains

  1. Open the Grains panel and choose Find grains.
  2. Check the signal profile. For CL, choose brighter or darker to include CL-dark grains.
  3. Review dimmed artifacts and their reasons. Choose Keep as grain when a flagged object is a grain.

Grain numbers stay stable across re-runs. Grains also captured by a neighbouring area appear dashed in that area’s colour. Find missed grains adds uncovered detections while keeping existing outlines, numbers and spot links; added outlines are dashed until accepted.

Edit outlines

Choose a tool, then work on the grain in the image.

ToolKeyWhat it does
DeleteDClick a grain to remove it. The grain card also has Not a grain and Delete grain.
OutlineODraw a lasso, freehand or click by click. It snaps to grain edges; hold ⌘ for no snap. Close with a double-click, Enter or the first point. A new outline replaces the grain under it; Shift adds to that grain, Option cuts from it.
BrushBPaint a grain larger at a size in µm; Option erases.
+Grain, SplitG, KAdd a grain at a click; split touching grains by drawing a line.

Find again in the current area keeps hand-drawn grains. Finding again in every area warns that hand edits will be replaced. Use ⌘Z to undo and Shift + ⌘Z to redo.

Place spots

  1. Choose Spot with S, then click a grain.
  2. Set the diameter in µm in the toolbar or Spot entry. Arrow steps are 1 µm, Shift steps 5 µm and Option steps 0.5 µm. Typed decimals are kept; 1–6 choose presets.
  3. Check the next name before placing. The default pattern is {sample}_{n}; for example, Sample-A_1.

Within a session, n counts up across samples and areas. Type Next n to start elsewhere, or enable restart at 1 for each sample. Spot entry warns when the next name is already used.

Drag a spot to move it and double-click its name to rename it. Spot size comes only from the size input in the side panel, for new and selected spots; placed spots are never resized by dragging. Hold Option for free placement without snapping.

Edit several items

⌘-click toggles selection. Shift-click selects a table range or adds on the canvas; Shift-drag on empty space selects a rectangle. ⌘A selects all in the area and Esc clears selection.

Change diameter, use Renumber… or Sample or pattern…, or delete the selection. Renumber previews the order and checks duplicate names. Each batch action is one undo step.

Save and export

Export spots as CSV or GeoJSON from the Spots panel, and grains with Grain CSV. Save the project with ⌘S. In a browser, Save downloads a .gaproj; desktop saves beside the package or back to the project file or folder it came from.

With an optional UPbDB connection and linked mount, stored results appear on spots. The Laser panel fits stage coordinates from fiducial spots and exports a generic spot list; see the developer reference.
