Area screen

Stitch and tone

Join the tiles, inspect the overlaps and choose the image tone.

  1. Select an area in the list. Before stitching, tiles appear at their stage positions.
  2. Choose Stitch all areas, or Stitch for the selected area.
  3. Choose Auto under Tone to set background leveling, stretch and CLAHE from the histogram.
  4. Inspect the mosaic, then choose Open canvas or Find grains.

Adjust tone

Black and White set the displayed range; Gamma adjusts the middle tones. Low % and High % set the stretch percentiles. CLAHE adjusts local contrast. Changes to correction and tone re-render the mosaic live.

Before / After ⇄ compares Auto’s result with the state before Auto. Reset levels resets black, white and gamma; Reset returns correction, blending and tone to defaults.

Check the stitch

Turn on tiles to see tile frames and residuals to see overlap measurements. The summary reports residual RMS and maximum in px. Click a tile for its metadata, stage position and drift.

Correction controls adjust gain and offset, background leveling, beam-exposure steps and blending. Tile overrides let you change one tile’s gain and offset; Match neighbours clears its override. Changes to the stitching threshold require Re-stitch.

Match grain tone

After finding grains, enable equalize grains to match their median and spread to the area or a chosen grain. Adjust strength and the soft edge in µm.

Move around the image

The mouse wheel zooms about the cursor; two-finger scroll pans and pinch zooms. Drag empty space to pan and double-click to fit. Area, Canvas and Layout previews use the same gestures.
