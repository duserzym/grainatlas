GrainAtlas

Stitch SEM tiles, outline grains, place laser spots and make print sheets.

Work with CL, BSE and SE grain-mount images in the macOS app, a browser or UPb Lab. Read packages locally and connect to UPbDB when you need to share an atlas or see results.

Download for macOSRead the manual

What it does

Open your images

Read a package, one area or loose images with a known scale.

Stitch and tone

Join tiles from stage positions and adjust the mosaic with live tone controls.

Grains and spots

Find grains, edit their outlines and name laser spots.

Print sheets

Keep neighbouring grains together on paper and export registration files.

Connect to UPbDB

Sign in, compare project changes and read results on spots.

Batch work

Stitch, segment, re-render and export from the command line.

Part of the lab workflow

UPb Lab opens the workflow in one place. GrainAtlas prepares images and spots; UPbDB stores sessions and links acquisitions to spots; UPbRPy reduces the session and submits results to UPbDB. Stored results can then be read beside the SEM images.

Open source

GrainAtlas is released under the MIT license. Sample data and test fixtures stay outside the public repository. See the screenshot policy for the manual images.
