Layout screen

Print sheets

Carry the mount’s spatial context to the laser session.

  1. Find and review grains on the Canvas, then open Layout.
  2. Choose Print sheets → Grain sheets. Set title, lab line and paper.
  3. Leave Scale empty to choose a scale from grain sizes, or enter a fixed number of µm per mm of paper.
  4. Choose Make print sheets and inspect each sheet with the preview arrows.

Keep neighbours together

A grain on paper is found by the grains around it, so automatic sheets keep continuous pieces of the mosaic together. Where a piece must be cut, a repeated strip preserves the neighbouring context.

Review the output

Sheets use one scale for the mount. Cross-area duplicates are left to their owner area. Flagged artifacts, grains below the size limit and strays are reported in the summary, along with checks for cut or repeated grains.

Adjust Smallest grain and Keep grains together within in µm when needed. The laser spot key uses the sizes you enter. Denoise, sharpen and background stripping affect exported images; a band keeps its background when stripping is unsafe.

Export and print

  • Export PDF writes the print sheets.
  • Registration saves the print_registration.json sidecar that maps bands back to the mosaic. Keep it with the PDF.
  • Export SVG saves individual pages.
  • Print… opens the browser print dialog. Allow its pop-up, or use the PDF.

Check the scale bar with a ruler on the printed page before using it for registration.

Other layouts

Whole area, one sheet prints a continuous stitched area without requiring segmentation. Choose area, paper, orientation, scale and whether to show a grid. Grains or areas provides the separate layout for whole areas or individual grain crops.
